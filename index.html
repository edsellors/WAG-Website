About the group

Dedicated consultant-led anaesthetic services across Worcestershire.

Worcestershire Anaesthetic Group LLP is a distinguished Limited Liability Partnership of 17 consultant anaesthetists, dedicated to providing top-tier anaesthetic services in Worcestershire. All consultant members are currently employed by the NHS at Worcestershire Acute Hospitals NHS Trust, bringing together expertise from both the private sector and the NHS.

The group offers a diverse range of anaesthetic services, tailored to the varied needs of patients with professionalism and precision. This commitment to clinical excellence has established Worcestershire Anaesthetic Group LLP as a trusted partner for over 50 consultant surgeons in private practice within Worcestershire.

Central to the group’s practice is a consultant on-call service, reflecting a strong commitment to patient safety and round-the-clock care.
